Inhibition of Return Experiment

Instructions

Welcome to the Inhibition of Return Experiment!

Your task is to respond as quickly as possible when you see the green go-signal. Here's how it works:

  • You will see a fixation point (white dot) in the center
  • Two boxes will appear on the left and right sides
  • A cue (visual signal) will appear in one of the boxes
  • After a delay, a go-signal will appear in either the same box (cued) or the other box (uncued)
  • Press A for stimuli on the LEFT side
  • Press L for stimuli on the RIGHT side
  • Response speed and accuracy are important

There will be 2 blocks of 50 trials each (100 trials total). Click "Continue" to begin.
